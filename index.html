Calculates minimum defensible sample sizes for testing Control Operating Effectiveness (COE) based on attribute sampling.

Parameters

Sampling Methodology Rationale:

Under a 95% confidence level with a 5% Tolerable Deviation Rate (TDR) and 0 expected exceptions, evaluating 60 items provides statistical assurance that the true control exception rate is below 5%.

Private Wealth Control Testing Procedures

Pre-built 2LoD testing procedures for key private banking operational risk controls.

High-Value Wire Voice Verification

High Risk

Control to prevent unauthorized fund transfers initiated via email or unverified channels.

CDE Criteria: Policy mandates independent call-back to registered contact number for orders > $250k.

COE Test: Inspect voice logging system; confirm call timestamp predates execution and phone number matches core static registry.

DPM Mandate Drift Monitoring

Medium Risk

Detecting unauthorized asset allocation drift in Discretionary Portfolio Management.

CDE Criteria: Daily automated breach alert when equity/fixed-income bucket drifts > 5% from mandate.

COE Test: Validate ticket resolution within 48 hours for sampled breach logs; confirm written client notifications.

Cross-Border RM Activity Logging

High Risk

Ensuring Relationship Managers do not solicit securities in non-licensed foreign jurisdictions.

CDE Criteria: Pre-travel clearance approval required in CRM prior to overseas client interactions.

COE Test: Reconcile RM expense travel logs with CRM pre-approvals and country manual limits.

Lombard Loan Collateral Revaluation

Medium Risk

Preventing unhedged gap risk on margin loans secured against volatile single-stock collateral.

CDE Criteria: Real-time pricing feeds must trigger automated margin calls at LTV > 70%.

COE Test: Sample market-drop days; verify automated margin notices were dispatched without manual suppression.
