Private Wealth Control Testing Playbooks

2LoD and 3LoD control test procedures with standard audit benchmark sampling and acceptance criteria.

High-Value Wire Voice Verification

Process: Tier 1 Critical Outflow | Benchmark: n=25-45, k=0
Critical

Control to prevent fraudulent or unauthorized fund transfers initiated via unverified channels.

CDE Test: Dual-authorization system gate enforced for > $250k; independent callback to verified core registry number.

CPE Sampling: AICPA Attribute sampling of 25–45 executed transfers ($k=0$). Reconcile voice recording time against swift dispatch timestamp.

DPM Mandate Drift Monitoring

Process: Tier 2 Operational | Benchmark: n=40-55, k=1
Medium Risk

Detecting portfolio allocation drift outside client investment mandate boundaries.

CDE Test: Daily automated batch report for > 5% asset class deviation with mandatory sign-off workflow.

CPE Sampling: Sample 45 daily batch runs allowing up to 1 non-material delay ($k=1$) if underlying portfolio rebalancing occurred within SLA.

Cross-Border RM Travel Pre-Clearance

Process: Tier 2 Operational | Benchmark: n=25-45, k=1
Medium Risk

Ensuring Relationship Managers do not solicit clients in foreign jurisdictions without licensing clearance.

CDE Test: Mandatory pre-travel CRM approval linked to country manual limits prior to travel expense reimbursement.

CPE Sampling: Sample 35 travel records across private banking desks; reconcile travel expense dates with pre-approval tickets.

PEP / High-Risk Account Periodic Review

Process: Tier 1 Critical Compliance | Benchmark: 100% (n=1-2/yr)
Critical

Annual KYC / Source of Wealth re-assessment for high-risk and PEP wealth clients.

CDE Test: Core banking hard-stop prevents account trading/transfers if annual review is overdue past 30 days.

CPE Sampling: Low-frequency benchmark: 100% review of annual triggers (or minimum 25 accounts if high-volume). Zero deviations allowed.
