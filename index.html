Private Wealth Control Testing Playbooks

Standardized testing playbooks with explicit execution frequencies.

High-Value Wire Callback Verification

Frequency: Ad-Hoc / Event-Driven (> $250k)
High Risk

Control to prevent unauthorized fund transfers initiated via email or unverified channels.

CDE Criteria: Policy mandates independent call-back to registered contact number for orders > $250k.

COE Sampling: Sample 25–45 transactions across all wealth booking desks; test voice recording against core registry.

DPM Mandate Drift Monitoring

Frequency: Daily Automated Batch
Medium Risk

Detecting unauthorized asset allocation drift in Discretionary Portfolio Management.

CDE Criteria: Daily automated breach alert when equity/fixed-income bucket drifts > 5% from mandate.

COE Sampling: Sample 25 daily drift reports across the year; verify tickets resolved within 48h SLA.

Cross-Border RM Travel Pre-Clearance

Frequency: Ad-Hoc / Pre-Trip
High Risk

Ensuring Relationship Managers do not solicit securities in non-licensed foreign jurisdictions.

CDE Criteria: Pre-travel clearance approval required in CRM prior to overseas client interactions.

COE Sampling: Sample 25 business trips; reconcile RM flight/hotel expense claims with approved CRM travel filings.

PEP / High-Risk Account Periodic Review

Frequency: Annual / Bi-Annual
High Risk

Periodic Know-Your-Customer (KYC) and Source of Wealth (SoW) re-assessment for high-risk clients.

CDE Criteria: Core banking hard-stop blocks transactions if annual review date passes without formal re-approval.

COE Sampling: Test 100% of PEP accounts overdue > 30 days; confirm system restriction was applied.

Control Deficiency Evaluators

Independent evaluation modules for Control Design Effectiveness (CDE) and Control Operating Effectiveness (COE).

1. Control Design Effectiveness (CDE) Evaluator

Evaluates whether the control as documented is conceptually adequate to mitigate the target risk.

Design Assessment
CDE Rating
SUB-OPTIMAL DESIGN

Design Assessment Rationale:

The control relies on manual intervention with non-automated checks. Risk of human error or delayed detection is heightened.

Assurance Rule: If CDE is rated Deficient, COE testing cannot compensate for fundamental design flaws.

2. Control Operating Effectiveness (COE / Performance) Evaluator

Evaluates execution consistency calibrated against Control Frequency.

Performance Assessment
COE Rating
OPERATING EFFECTIVELY

Operating Performance Rationale:

0 exceptions observed across the independent testing sample. Full audit trail verified with timely escalation handling.

Frequency Sensitivity: Low frequency controls (Annual/Quarterly) have 0 error tolerance; a single failure represents a significant population breakdown.
